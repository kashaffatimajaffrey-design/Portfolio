This narrative blends real-life mental health challenges with a story that breaks the fourth wall, inviting readers to question their own perceptions.

The novel follows characters navigating the fragile boundaries of identity and consciousness, drawing from psychological research to authentically portray the experience of dissociative identity disorder and the manipulative dynamics within cult environments.

Both works explore complex themes through immersive storytelling and character development, reflecting my interest in the intersection of psychology, society, and human behavior.
